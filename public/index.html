And on your phone?

Pair with the KDE Connect app ↗. Enable clipboard sharing on both devices.

Android

Computer → phone
Text arrives automatically while connected.
Phone → computer
On Android 10+, tap Send Clipboard in KDE Connect. Automatic sending needs a workaround.
Android clipboard details ↗

iPhone / iPad

Computer → phone
Text arrives while KDE Connect is open and connected.
Phone → computer
Tap Push Clipboard in the app. iOS limits background connections.
iOS limitations ↗

Both official phone clients support text clipboard only. Use file sharing to send photos or files to a phone.

On your Mac

Right in the menu bar.

A small badge shows how many paired devices are connected. A × means none.

2 connected
None connected

See device status and battery at a glance. Open a device’s menu to send a file or use its other features.

Logs when you need them.

Diagnostic logging is off by default. Turn it on in Preferences or the Diagnostics menu to help troubleshoot.
