YOUR DEVICES, TOGETHER

A Mac on one side.
More possibilities
on the other.

Soduto is a macOS client for KDE Connect. Pair it with a compatible Linux or Android device on your local network.

Read the compatibility notes
Your MacmacOS 14+
Your devicesKDE Connect

Text, images & single files
Images and files need a compatible clipboard peer. Transfers are limited to 50 MiB.

Using GNOME?
Official GSConnect syncs text only. See the GSConnect fork for image/file clipboard work.

Paste support varies by app and desktop. Ubuntu GNOME/Wayland image/file sync still needs real-device validation. Folders and multiple-file selections aren’t supported.
